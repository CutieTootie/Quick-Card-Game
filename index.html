How to play

  1. You and the computer take turns placing one card from your hand into one of your empty zones. You have 6 front zones and 6 back zones.
  2. After you place a card you draw a new one.
  3. When all 24 zones are full, every card fights the opposing card in the same spot: front fights front, back fights back, column by column.
  4. The higher power wins that battle and scores 1 point. Equal power is a tie and nobody scores. Most points after 12 battles wins.
